## Lexical Resources

### Greek New Testament Lexicons

**BDAG** — Bauer, W., Danker, F.W., Arndt, W.F., & Gingrich, F.W. *A Greek-English Lexicon of the New Testament and Other Early Christian Literature*, 3rd ed. University of Chicago Press, 2000.
- The standard academic Greek NT lexicon. Commercial work; AI approximates its methodology and entry structure.
- [Publisher](https://press.uchicago.edu/)

**Thayer's Greek Lexicon** — Thayer, J.H. *A Greek-English Lexicon of the New Testament*. 1889. Public domain.
- Available at [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/thayer/greek) and [BibleHub](https://biblehub.com/lexicon/)

**LSJ** — Liddell, H.G., Scott, R., Jones, H.S. *A Greek-English Lexicon*. 9th ed. Oxford, 1940. Public domain.
- Available at [Perseus Digital Library](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/resolveform?redirect=true&lang=Greek)

### Hebrew Old Testament Lexicons

**HALOT** — Koehler, L., Baumgartner, W., Richardson, M.E.J. (tr.). *The Hebrew and Aramaic Lexicon of the Old Testament*. Brill, 2001.
- The standard academic Hebrew OT lexicon. Commercial work; AI approximates its methodology.
- [Publisher](https://brill.com/)

**BDB** — Brown, F., Driver, S.R., Briggs, C.A. *A Hebrew and English Lexicon of the Old Testament*. 1906. Public domain.
- Available at [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/brown/hebrew) and [BibleHub](https://biblehub.com/hebrew/)

**Gesenius' Hebrew Grammar** — Gesenius, W. (Kautzsch ed., Cowley tr.). 1910. Public domain.
- Available at [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/geseniushebrewgr00geseuoft)

### Concordances

**Strong's Exhaustive Concordance** — Strong, J. 1890. Public domain.
- Available at [BibleHub](https://biblehub.com/strongs/) and many other sites.

## Scholarly Integrity Notice

The AI commentary engine (powered by Google Gemini with Google Search grounding) is instructed to cite from the sources above. However:

1. **AI-generated lexical entries approximate BDAG/HALOT methodology** — they are not substitutes for consulting the published lexicons in a formal academic context.
2. **Quotes are generated with citation intent**, but you should verify any direct quote against the original text before relying on it in serious scholarship.
3. **Google Search grounding** improves factual accuracy but does not guarantee that retrieved sources are authoritative. CCEL, Perseus, and Archive.org links are preferred over general web results.
4. **This application is a study aid**, not a replacement for formal theological education or peer-reviewed commentary. Use it as a starting point, not a final reference.
